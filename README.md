🚗 Car Game – Basic Information

A car game is a video game where the player controls a car and drives it on different roads or tracks.

Main features:

🏎️ Car Control: Move the car left, right, forward, and backward.
🛣️ Racing: Race against other cars or try to complete a track quickly.
🏁 Levels: Different levels can have different tracks and difficulties.
⭐ Score: Players can earn points by completing levels, collecting items, or winning races.
🚧 Obstacles: Roads can contain obstacles that the player must avoid.
🔧 Car Selection: Players can choose different cars with different speeds and abilities.
